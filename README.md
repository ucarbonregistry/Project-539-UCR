The project activity is titled- “15.65 MW Bundled Wind Energy Project in India by Intech Group”.
It is a wind-power Project which is spread across villages of Sakri in Maharashtra & villages in
Bhelahadi Karnataka state in India. The project activity involves the wind bundle Sr. No 01
which is owned by Inno Automation. (Project Proponent or PP) having the total installed
capacity of 2.5 MW and consists of 2 Wind turbine generators (WTGs) each of 1.25 MW
capacity in Dhule district of Maharashtra. 

The wind bundle Sr. No 02 is owned by Intech
Automation – (Project Proponent or PP) The total installed capacity is 2.5 MW wind power
project in dhule of Maharashtra. PP consists of 2 WTGs of 1.25 MW each. The wind bundle Sr.
No 03 Intech Power Kovai is the total installed capacity is 1.25 MW and consists of one WTG in
Dhule district of Maharashtra.

The wind farm bundle Sr. No 04 is owned by Intech Power Chennai pvt ltd – (Project Proponent
or PP) having 2 states in that Maharashtra & Karnataka in which the total capacity is 9.4 MW.
Maharashtra having 6.25 MW & Karnataka 3.15 MW.

The bundled wind power projects, owned by Inno Automation, Intech Automation, Intech Power
Kovai, and Intech Power Chennai Pvt Ltd, aim to generate clean electricity through renewable
wind energy. Project activity consists of 24 WEGs (1.25) kw (MH) & 0.225 kw (KA) of Suzlon
make S-66. The project is owned by Inno Automation, Intech Automation, Intech Power Kovai,
and Intech Power Chennai Pvt Ltd.

The project involves the supply, erection, commissioning, and operation of these machines,
managed by Suzlon, which serves as the equipment supplier and Operation and Maintenance
contractor.

The wind farm generates approximately 79,236.97 MWh of clean electricity in the current
monitoring period. The generated electricity is supplied to the state electricity utility MSEDCL &
KPTCL under a long-term power purchase agreement (PPA). which is part of the INDIAN
(Northern, Eastern, Western and North Eastern) grid (now merged in the integrated Indian grid)
in India, under a long-term power purchase agreement (PPA) and business transfer agreements
signed between the following:

On September 1, 2020, Inno Automation India Pvt Ltd entered into Business Transfer
Agreements (BTAs) with two sellers: Palms Enercon Inc (Seller Location: G006) and Chawala
Brothers Pvt Ltd (Seller Location: K295) for the acquisition of their respective business assets.
Substation for Maharashtra turbine Jamde/220kv

Furthermore, a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) was executed between Maharashtra

The expected operational lifetime of the project is for 20 years. Addressing the energy demandsupply gap in Maharashtra and Karnataka and supporting the region’s sustainable growth.

The first WEC under the project activity was commissioned on 30/06/2005 and the last WEC
under the project activity was commissioned on 13/08/2008.The project has been operational
since the earliest commissioning date.
The project activity has not been registered or applied for voluntary carbon benefits under any
other GHG program. PP seeks verification under UCR from 01/09/2020 onwards, i.e., crediting
period for UCR starts from 01/09/2020 to 31/12/2024. Hence, there is no double counting for the
said project.
